# Vishnu Dharmam - 04 - Dhaanam

![](images/01-aec967-f3f843d984d3407e9e294e72406cda26.jpg)

In our previous entry, we discussed the concept of Bhakti. Now we shall see Dhaanam ( giving charity to those who are in dire need ). It is said that Dhaanam is of many types. It could be in the following forms: *1) Vidya/Saraswathi Dhaanam:* imparting knowledge in the form of *Bhagavat Vishyam* (preaching the message of the Lord and His stories), or in the form of *Bhaagavata Vishayam* (preaching the message of the great Acharyas and people who spent their lives with an intense devotion to the Lord), or in the form of *Vipareetha Vishayam* (giving any new knowledge to anyone who does not possess it). This kind of Dhaanam is considered to be one of the greatest Dhaanam that one can provide. *2) Swarna Dhaanam:* Donating gold. Giving gold to a person who requires it, is held in very high esteem according to the shastras. This could be because the person requires gold as it is or as a form of monetary exchange. In modern-day, the best way to donate gold would be to donate gold to those who are incapable of buying gold to make a *managalya-thaali*. *3) Bhu/Prithvi Dhannam:* Giving a person some amount of land. *4) Go Dhaanam:* Giving a cow or a cow with its calf along with some fodder is said to bring high merit to the person performing the charity. It is said that performing *Go Dhaanam* is even more auspicious than performing *Swarna Dhaanam*. *5) Anna Dhaanam:* Feeding a person a meal is said to be the greatest Dhaanam that one can give. If one is **not capable** of performing any of the above dhaanam, but still wishes to reap the benefits of performing dhaanam, they are to chant ***Purusha-Suktam***forty times a day to receive the same benefits.
