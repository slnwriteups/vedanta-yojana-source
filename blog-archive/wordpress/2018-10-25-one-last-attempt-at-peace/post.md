# One Last Attempt at Peace

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

In an attempt to stop an impending war situation, Dhritarashtra chose to seek to advise from Bhishma. Bhishma replied it would be unwise to lock horns in a fight where the outcome is already known to be disastrous. Bhishma said Arjuna single-handedly beat the entire Kuru Army. Now that Shri Krishna is also by his side, victory will definitely be on the side of Pandavas. He then asked Dhritarashtra to counsel Duryodhana into giving back the Pandavas what is rightfully theirs. When Dhritarashtra tried Duryodhana in a fury questioned his father if his sons meant more to him or the Pandavas. He further said he would not give even a single needle head's worth of land to the Pandavas and stormed out of Dhritarashtra's chamber.

Shri Krishna told the Pandavas that he would for the last time visit the court of Hastinapur and try his hand in imploring reason to Duryodhana and try to make him realize that nothing good could possibly come out of a war. Shri Krishna arrived in Hastinapur and instead of staying in the palace chose to spend some time with Vidura. He stayed the night at Vidura's house and in the morning went to the court of Hastinapur to try and convince Duryodhana out of the idea of war. Krishna presented his argument asking that if the Kauravas did not want to return the entire kingdom then at least provide the Pandavas with five villages.

Duryodhana in a crimson fury snapped at Krishna saying that he was not qualified to come to the court with such an issue and told him to go back to herding cattle. He further told Krishna that he would not give even a single needle head's worth of land. He then instructed the guards to tie Krishna up and make him a prisoner. As the guards were tying Krishna, he started to grow to an immeasurable height and took his ***Vishwaroopam Form***. All the people present in court became eyewitnesses of the Lord's true form but were unable to comprehend what they had seen. Only Vidura and Bhishma could appreciate the Lord's true form. It is also said the Lord bestowed Dhritarashtra with a set of special eyes for him to be able to see his true form. Dhritarashtra saw the Vishwaroopam and immediately asked that he become blind again and not see anything else in his entire life as he had been blessed with this sight. Shri Krishna then assumed his human form and left the court back to the Pandavas and told them Duryodhana had made war an inevitable situation and asked them to harbor support.
