# Virata Nagari

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

 **देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

After completing their twelve years of vanavasa the Pandavas had to serve a year of agnatavasa ( incognito). During this one year, they were supposed to hide their weapons and stay in disguise of a commoner. They chose to take refuge in Matsya Pradesh, the kingdom of King Virata. All the Pandavas and Draupadi took roles such that they would be indistinguishable to the public. Yudhisthira took the guise of the king's advisor and his coach for the game of dice. Bhima took the guise of the head cook in the king's kitchen. Arjuna took the role of Brihanala (dance teacher) to Princess Uttara as Urvashi's curse took effect he became a eunuch for that year. Nakula and Sahadeva took the guise of a cattle-keeper and stable-keeper to the royal palace of Matsya. Draupadi took the guise of Sairandhri, a maid to Queen Sudheshana.

The army of Matsya Pradesh was run by Keechaka, the queen's brother. He once laid his eyes on Sairandhri and was immediately attracted to her beauty. He soon questioned his sister about her and got to know all about her. The next day he approached Sairandhri and asked her to marry him. As she refused, he tried to force himself upon her in front of a full court. Ashamed of what was happening, she approached Bheema to help her. They hatched a plan wherein Sairandhri would seduce Keechaka

and drag into the dance hall where Brihanala would be loudly practicing beats to a dance piece. Bhima would be in the guise of Sairandhri ready to attack Keechaka. As the plan unfolded, Keechaka entered the dancehall and came to see Sairandhri, Bheema jumped Keechaka and with his bare hands ripped Keechaka to shreds in such a way that the corpse wouldn't be recognizable. Soon the kingdom saw that Keechaka was missing and realized that had been killed. Soon the news of Keechaka's death spread across the entire Aaryrvrata and soon the news reached Hastinapur. Duryodhana and Karna went through the news and deduced the possible people who could have matched Keechaka in terms of strength and narrowed down that the people who could possibly kill Keechaka were Duryodhana, Dushasana, Karna, Balarama, and Bhima. After seeing all the evidence they narrowed down to the fact that among all the possible people Duryodhana, Dushasana and Karna were at Hastinapur and Balarama was at Dwaraka so the only person who could have killed him was Bheema. They immediately set their sights on the border of Matsya Pradesh and left with their army to attack so they could enforce another twelve years of Vanavasa and a thirteenth year of Agnathavasa on the Pandavas.
