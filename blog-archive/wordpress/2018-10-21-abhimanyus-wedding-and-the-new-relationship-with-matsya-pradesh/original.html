नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।


After the Virata Yudha, the Pandavas revealed themselves to Virata and sent a clear message to Hastinapur that they were a force to be reckoned with. 
Abhimanyu along with the Upa-Pandavas and Shri Krishna came to visit the Pandavas who had just finished their term of thirteen years of humiliation. The Pandavas and Draupadi had a very emotional reunion with their children.
Virata approached Arjuna who was the war hero who had saved Matsya Pradesh and his son Uttara Kumara and asked him to marry his daughter Uttara. Arjuna replied that he had once been a guru to Uttara and hence it would not be appropriate for him to get married to Uttara but proposed that she be married to Abhimanyu who was also a very capable groom for Uttara. 
So Abhimanyu got married to Uttara and Matsya Pradesh became allies with the Pandavas.
