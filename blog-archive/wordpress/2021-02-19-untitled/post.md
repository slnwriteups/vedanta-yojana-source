# (untitled)

I have chosen the advertisement for a Kit-Kat commercial initially aired in the Indian market about eight years ago. The caption of the ad was “Have a break, have a Kit Kat.” Nestle manufactures Kit-Kat. Nestle has been operating in India since March 1959. Nestle started to manufacture chocolates and confectionery in the year 1990. Nestle India's revenue at the end of 2019 is estimated to be close to $1.8 billion. Nestle Kit-Kat is one of the most successful brands globally, and every year, over 12 billion Nestle Kit-Kat fingers are consumed around the globe.

The advertisement opens to a scene wherein a professor is taking his students on a hospital tour, and one of the students looks at a baby playroom while opening a Kit-Kat. The student starts to eat his Kit-Kat and slowly starts to imagine a scenario of what the babies might be doing when nobody is keeping an eye on them. The advertisement proceeds to show a visual of the babies creating music and dancing. The student keeps watching the babies as he finishes his Kit-Kat. As he finishes, he notices that the babies are just playing around like normal. The message being conveyed by the ad is, breaks often offer opportunities that one may miss because of our busy lifestyle. Opportunities to see, hear, taste, feel or meet someone new and discover something. A good break can often put things in perspective, help you relax, and get you into the right mood and spirit to face whatever comes next.
