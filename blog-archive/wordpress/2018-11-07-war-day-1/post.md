# War- Day 1

नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

After Lord Shree Krishna gave Arjuna the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita war erupted. The first day of eighteen days began with Dushasana leading the front of the Kaurava army and Bheema lead the front of the Pandava army.
War erupted when in full flow. Bhishma was a force to be reckoned with, he was decimating the Pandava army without any mercy. Abhimanyu who was on standby while Bhishma was attacking could take no more and decided to take action. Abhimanyu instructed his charioteer to direct his chariot towards Pithamaha Bhishma. As instructed, the charioteer drove the chariot and intercepted Bhishma. As the two came face-to-face, the gods descended from the heavens to witness the feat of Parashurama trained Bhishma and Krishna trained Abhimanyu. Abhimanyu fired a series of arrows in front of Bhishma’s chariot and stopped it in its tracks. Bhishma was stunned seeing a sixteen year old boy in front of him. Nonetheless both of them had a great exchange of arrows with Abhimanyu uprooting the palm-tree flag of Bhishma. As soon as the flag was uprooted Duryodhana intervened and provided relief to Bhishma allowing him to focus his attention elsewhere. Before departing Bhishma marks Abhimanyu as a son worthy of Arjuna and the entire Kuru race.
In the meanwhile Uttara, son of Virata was facing off against Shalya and his army. Uttara was atop an elephant and was directing it to trample anything and everything that came in his way. Shalya sustained heavy losses. Once he came face- to- face with Uttara, he hurled a javelin into Utarra’s chest which impaled him and killed him. This did not deter the elephant from continuing its rampage. To stop the elephant from further trampling any more of his soldiers, Shalya drew his sword and cut of the elephant’s trunk causing the elephant to fall in pain and die. Uttara‘s brother Sveta who was overlooking this battle went into a fury upon seeing the death of his brother. Sveta immediately engaged Shalya in shower of arrows. Sveta defended himself valiantly and soon the battle drew to a point where Sveta was overpowering Shalya. Seeing this Dushasana immediately sent relief forces to assist Shalya but Sveta made quick work of the relief unit as well. Bhishma was alerted of the situation and made his way to face Sveta. The both of them engaged in an incessant shower of arrows with neither side giving an inch to the other. When Dushasana saw this he came along and struck Sveta’s chariot. Bhishma then gained the upper hand and used this opportunity to kill Sveta. No sooner than Sveta was killed was the sun going down. Dushasana blew his conch signifying the closure of the first day of war.
The Pandava army suffered huge losses on the first day. Yushishthira started to get really worried by the unchecked onslaught of Pithamaha Bhishma. Krishna then consoled Yudhishthira saying the Pandava army still had great warriors in their ranks in the form of Satyaki, Virata, Drupada and Drishtadyumna besides Krishna himself.
