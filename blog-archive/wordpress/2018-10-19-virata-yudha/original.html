नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

Duryodhana decided to attack Virata with the objectives: 1) To the natch the cows, gems, and gold of king Virata 2) To subjugate Virata and increase the sphere of influence and 3) To oblige Trigarta king Susharma who was Duryodhana’s friend and was earlier harassed by Kichaka in service of Virata. Kurus had no special enmity towards Virata. They were prepared to face weakened Virata and not Arjuna full of enthusiasm, anger and desire to humiliate Kauravas.

When Arjuna in the disguise of Brihannala ran after prince Uttara to bring him back, some of the Kauravas doubted if he was Arjuna. Bhishma, Drona, etc got worried about the safety of Duryodhana. Divide between Orava army came to fore with Karna criticizing Drona, but Duryodhana forwarded the logic “If he is not Arjuna he will be killed by us and if he is Arjuna Pandavas will have to go back to the forest once more.”

When Arjuna retrieved his weapons from Shami tree and installed his ‘Kapidhwaja’ on the chariot and blew Conch Devadutta, Kaurava army got scared, while Duryodhana was encouraging them, Karna’s criticism of Drona and self-aggrandizement exposed divide Kaurava army once again, Kripacharya and Ashvatthama siding Dronacharya and threatened to retire from the battlefield. Bhishma and Duryodhana somehow made Karna apologize and patched the divide. During the discussion, Bhishma explained how Pandavas have more than completed period of 12+1 years and won’t have to undergo the torture again. On the advice of Bhishma, cows were dispatched to Hastinapur with 1/4 the army, with another 1/4 the king Duryodhana was sent back to Hastinapura. Half the army with Bhishma, Drona, Kripa, Ashvatthama, Karna, etc remained to engage Arjuna. Kauravas already accepted defeat mentally.

Arjuna had clear objectives. He observed that Duryodhana is not in the army facing him and conjectured that he must be taking away cows to Hastinapura. He chased and attacked the army protecting the cows. With scared soldiers trying to face Arjuna, cows started running back towards Viratanagari. Arjuna then quickly attacked and played havoc with retreating Duryodhana’s army killing king Shatruntapa and Karna’s brother Sangamajeet. He then made Karna retreat, when returned with Drona, Kripa, Bhishma, and Kaurava brothers, his chariot was destroyed and was made to retreat once again. Then Arjuna defeated Kripa. Before shooting arrows at Drona, Arjuna first bowed to Guru and waited till Drona started shooting arrows, the fierce battle Karna ensued and Drona had to retreat followed by Ashvatthama. Arjuna’s arrows penetrated Karna’s famous mail, injured and defeated him once again. Then Arjuna fought Bhishma protected by Kaurava princes and defeated them all, along with Duryodhana. Finally, after defeating them comprehensively, Arjuna used Sanmohanaastra depriving Kaurava army of their senses. Arjuna ordered Uttara to take away garments of Kaurava warriors for his sister. Defeated Kauravas decided to retreat and Arjuna saluted departing Bhishma, Drona, Kripa, Ashvatthama, Balhika, Somadatta and stuck down Duryodhana’s crown with arrows.

Arjuna behaved like a hero. He stopped Uttara from leaving the battlefield. Got and worshipped his weapons and flag. Declared his identity before shooting arrows and expressed respect to Guru Drona. After defeating Kaurava army comprehensively, retrieved cows and took away their precious garments for princess Uttara. Although he defeated all Kaurava warriors, it was Duryodhana and Karna whom he defeated repeatedly and comprehensively. Finally, he saluted respected one’s and permitted afflicted soldiers to go back.
