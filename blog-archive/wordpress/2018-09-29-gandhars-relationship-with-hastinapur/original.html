नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् । 
देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

During Vichitravirya's rule as king, Bhishma was expanding the borders, he encountered the border of Gandhara Pradesh. Bhishma ruthlessly crushed the king of Gandhara in war and captured him along with his one hundred sons. It is said that after capturing the hundred and one individuals a single meal was provided enough to feed a single individual. The king of Gandhara chose to give that meal to the smartest son among his hundred children. That single individual, as a result, survived while the others slowly perished one after the other. This individual would later be known to us Shakuni the uncle to the Kauravas. On their deathbed, Shakuni's brothers and father asked that he take their bones and make dice out of them as he was an expert in Dyuth-Krida (game of dice). These dice would always listen to whatever he'd say as they were made as a result of the great sacrifice. After Shakuni's survival, Bhishma released him and let him rule Gandhara Pradesh. As time passed Shakuni was fueled only by his thought of revenge, of how he could get back at Bhishma and the Kuru clan for the embarrassment that he had to bear. On one fine day news reached him that Dhritarashtra the blind prince of Hastinapur was looking for a bride. Without any second thought, he immediately sent news that his sister Gandhari would be married to Dhritarashtra. Upon marriage, she found out that Dhritarashtra was blind and hence she chose to remain blindfolded to share the same fate as her husband.
