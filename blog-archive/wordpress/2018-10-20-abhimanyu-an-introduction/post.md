# Abhimanyu an introduction

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

**देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

Before moving forward in the story I will take this opportunity to introduce a very important character who will play a crucial role in the war to come. The character being discussed is Abhimanyu.

Abhimanyu was the son of Arjuna and Subadhra(Lord Krishna's sister). He was a brave warrior who gave the best warriors trouble.

He had the knowledge of the Chakravyuha even before he was born. The Chakravyuha is a formation in a war where the soldiers are placed in the form of concentric circles and the most important warrior is placed in the center and the enemy must penetrate from the outermost circle and aim to reach the center.

Abhimanyu received the knowledge of the Chakravyuha as Arjuna was explaining it to Subadhra. As a baby in the womb, Abhimanyu used to listen with earnest to the tactics of war that Arjuna used to tell Subadhra. On one such occasion, he was listening on to the tactics of entering the Chakravyuha when Subadhra fell asleep and so he obtained only half the knowledge required to save his life in the future.

Abhimanyu was born in Dwaraka and lived with his mother and uncles Sri Krishna and Balarama who taught him all the nacks of war. As a young boy Lord Krishna trained Abhimanyu in archery. He grew up to become a very skilled archer like his father. He used to train with Lord Krishna's Narayanee Sainya. After the Virata Yudha Abhimanyu got married to Uttara the Princess of Matsya Pradesh. His son would be the sole survivor of the Kuru race and carry on the legacy of the Pandavas.
