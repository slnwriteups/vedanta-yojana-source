# Amba

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

**देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

After the vow taken up by Devavrata, Satyavati's father agreed to give his daughter's hand in marriage to King Shantanu. After their marriage, a few years passed by and Satyavati gave birth to her first child Chitrangada and in the subsequent years to follow gave birth to her second child Vichitravirya. Chitrangada went on to become a very capable warrior and had inherited the throne after the untimely death of Shantanu.

Briefly, after his inheritance of the throne, his fame brought a Gandharva to his palace gates. The Gandharva had an intention to take the throne of Hastinapur for himself. To stop this from happening Chitrangada sacrificed himself to protect the throne. As he didn't have any children to succeed him Vichitravirya became the successor to the throne. Soon after inheriting the throne Vichitravirya came of age to be married but as he wasn’t a capable warrior, Bhishma was instructed by Satyavati to find a suitable bride for his marriage. At the same time news had reached Hastinapur that the king of Kashi was organizing a *Swayamwara Ceremony* for his three daughters Amba, Ambalika and Ambika. Bhishma was sent to the court of Kashi as a representative of the throne of Hastinapur to win the three princesses in the *Swayamwara Ceremony* for his half-brother Vichitravirya. As Bhishma entered the royal court of Kashi, he made an announcement to all the suitors present at the ceremony that he had come as a representative of Hastinapur and that he was going to kidnap the princesses for his half-brother and if anyone was capable of stopping him were free to try. One after the other different kings tried their hand at trying to challenge and win the princesses but only in vain. Upon reaching Hastinapur Ambika and Ambalika accepted Bhishma’s terms and agreed to get married to Vichitravirya but Amba promptly refused to say she was in love with Salva, the king of Saubala and that she was brought to Hastinapur against her will. Hearing this Bhishma allows her to go back to Salva. Upon reaching Saulaba Salva tells Amba that he cannot marry her as she was abducted by Bhishma and it would be a humiliation to get married to her. After hearing this Amba returns to Hastinapur.

She narrates the events that took place and tells Bhishma that he is to marry her. Bhishma tells her that he is bound to his vow to stay a brahmacharya for his life and that he had made his intentions clear at the royal court that he was only representing his half-brother. Hearing this Amba storms out of Hastinapur in a fury and seeks refuge to Lord Parshurama, asking him to help her in this situation as Bhishma was his disciple. Upon hearing Amba’s plea Lord Parshurama agreed to help her and discipline Bhishma. Lord Parashurama tries to talk reason to Bhishma but only in vain. Finally, Lord Parshurama retorted to war. Bhishma and Lord Parshurama fought for twenty-three days. Their battle was very evenly matched with neither side giving an inch. After the twenty-third day, both sides had exhausted their normal weapons and only had divine weapons left in their respective *Astra-bhandhaar*. To avoid catastrophes Indra intervened and said this battle wasn’t going to decide anything. He said Bhishma was bound to his promise given to his father and he should honor it and Amba should stop her stop her attempt to force Bhishma to marry her.

Amba felt further insulted and took to the Himalayas to retort to penance to Lord Shiva who’s decision couldn’t be over-ruled by anyone. Finally, when her penance was answered by Lord Shiva he told her that he was impressed by her penance and has appeared to grant her a boon. Amba asked that she be responsible to kill Bhishma. Hearing Amba’s request Lord Shiva said that it wouldn’t be possible for her in her current life to take revenge against Bhishma but in her next life she would be responsible for Bhishma’s death and disappeared. Amba was overcome with joy and was eager to enter her next life.

Amba made a grand pyre and gave her soul as a sacrifice to Agni(the god of fire) and jumped into the pyre to be reborn in a later time as Shikhandi to King Drupada.
