# Drona and Drupada

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

**देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

With an introduction to Lord Shri Krishna now we will move forward on the journey of this wonderful story. We all know that Arjuna became the world's greatest archer by the teachings of his guru Drona. Today we will see the story of Drona.

Drona was the son of Sage Bharadwaja and studied under him. During the course of his studies, he met Drupada who was at the time the prince of Panchala. They became very close friends so much so that Drupada had told Drona that he would give him half of his kingdom when he became king. After completing his graduation, Drupada returned back to Panchala and soon assumed the throne. Drona married Kripi, sister of Kripacharya who was an advisor at the royal court of Hastinapur. Soon Drona and Kripi had a son named Aswatthama. Soon Drona was faced with the problem of poverty. The situation kept getting worse and it reached a situation wherein he could barely feed his family a single square meal. In this time of crisis, Kripi advised Drona to visit his friend Drupada and approach him for help. Drona visited the court of Panchala but he didn't quite receive the reception he was expecting. Drupada had forgotten all about Drona. When Drona persisted and reminded Drupada of all their childhood memories, Drupada blatantly refused and said friendship cannot exist between a beggar and a king. Drona stomaching all the insult asked that as a king he have mercy on a poor brahmin and at least be presented with a cow to sustain his family, but Drupada mercilessly showed him the door. Soon Bhishma recruited Drona as a teacher to the Pandavas and Kauravas. He taught all of the hundred and five children but Arjuna became his favorite student. He taught Arjuna many different techniques in archery and also transferred all of his knowledge of the celestial Divya-astras to him. Soon a time came when all his students graduated. It was custom to give Guru-Dakshina to a guru after the student's education was complete. Drona used this opportunity to avenge the humiliation he faced at the hands of Drupada.

Drona directed his students in the direction of Panchala and told them to bring Drupada in chains as a form of gurudakshina. Drupad's army proved no match to the might of the Pandavas who single-handedly defeated Drupada and his army and brought him back to Drona as requested. Drona reminded Drupada how he humiliated him when he approached him for help. He pointed out to Drupada that his country Panchala now belonged to him, having been offered as Guru Dakshina by his disciple Arjuna. He recalled Drupada's words that friendship could exist only between equals and pointed out that having been deprived of his country, Drupada was not fit to be Drona's friend! Yet, Drona said that he was magnanimously returning half of his country Panchala to him, retaining the other half for himself. Drona then released Drupada and sent him back after giving him half of the country back

Now Drupada had suffered a humiliation. He wanted to avenge his humiliation. But he had the wisdom to differentiate between Drona, who perpetrated the humiliation on him and Arjuna, who was only used as a weapon. So, he had no feelings of resentment or anger towards Arjuna. On the other hand, he admired Arjuna's valor and wanted to bring Arjuna to his side.
Drupada performed a ***Yagna*** and begot as fruits of this sacrifice, a son Drishtadhyumna who would kill Drona in a battle and a daughter Draupadi who would marry Arjuna.
