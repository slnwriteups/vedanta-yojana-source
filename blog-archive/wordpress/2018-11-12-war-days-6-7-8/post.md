# War- Days 6,7,8

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

On the sixth day of war the two armies again took the same formations as the previous day. Bheema penetrated through the formation and attacked Duryodhana with an intent to kill but Bhishma and others interfered and took Duryodhana to safety thus bringing a close to day six.

On day seven,there was fierce battle that was waged on the front once again with Kaurava pulling ahead. King Virata’s son Shanka was killed by Drona and the day ended with the Kauravas gaining the upper hand.

On day eight, Bheema went on a rampage and killed eighteen of the remaining ninety-two brothers. This crippled Duryodhana mentally and he became the target of the Pandavas. Arjuna’s son Iravan, born of the Naga-Kanya Uloopi perished in war at the hands of Alamvusha, a rakshasa sent by Duryodhana. The both of them fought an intense battle with Iravan loosing his life for the sake of his father and his kingdom. The Upapandavas, soon rushes to this scene and killed the rakshasa.
