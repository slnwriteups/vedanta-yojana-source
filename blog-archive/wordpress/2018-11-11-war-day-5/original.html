नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

As war broke out on the fifth day, war was being waged on different stages of the battlefield. Bhishma had arranged the Kauravas Army in the Makara-Vyuha (Alligator Formation) and the Pandavas Army arranged themselves in the Syena-Vyuha (Hawk Formation) and once again the slaughter began. Bhishma again went on an onslaught and killed hundreds of soldiers. Arjuna in reply killed many on the Kaurava side. On another side Satyaki was facing off against Drona who was effortlessly outclassing him. Drona at one point had Satyaki on the ropes when Bheema intervened and saved Satyaki from the jaws of death. Thus both sides suffered heavy losses on day five.
