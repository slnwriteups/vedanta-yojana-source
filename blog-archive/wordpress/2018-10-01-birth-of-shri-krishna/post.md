# Birth of Shri Krishna

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

**देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

The story of the Mahabharata is incomplete without Shri Krishna. Today lets look at the birth of Shri Krishna.

Shri Krishna was born to Devaki and Vasudev on the eighth day (Ashtami) during the krishna paksha( waxing phase of the moon ) in the month of Shraavan in Mathura.

Devaki was the daughter of King Ugrasena and sister of Kansa. Kansa was a very ambitious prince. In his ambition, he imprisoned his father and ascended the throne. As soon as he ascended the throne he started to expand the borders of his kingdom and to do so he sought matrimonial relationships with the daughter of the Magadha King, Jarasandha. With his matrimonial relationship to Magadha, Kansa's army grew extremely strong. His ambition did not end with conquering what was on Bhu-Lok(Plane of the earth) he wanted to conquer Indra-Lok. To accomplish this task Kansa started rallying demon support. He gathered demons such as Poothana, Agasura, Shakatasura, Trinavrath, Bakasura( not to be confused with the Bakasura killed by Bhima) and many others. With his gathered demon force he conquered Indra-Lok and forced Indra out of his own kingdom. Indra went to Lord Vishnu to implore him to help. Lord Vishnu said the time had come for him to reincarnate on the earth to eliminate sinners and re-establish the acts of dharma. Back in Mathura Kansa was looking for a groom for his sister Devaki and decided that he would give her hand in Vasudev a fellow member of the Yadava clan. On the day of their wedding after the ceremonies, Kansa decided that he'd personally drive the chariot to show Vasudev the city of Mathura. As the three of them boarded the chariot a voice emanated from the sky " Oh irreligious Kansa, your days of committing sins have been numbered. Your death is at hand. The eighth son Devaki and Vasudev will be your end. He will kill you!"

As soon as he heard this prophecy he attempted to kill Devaki. Vasudev intervened promising Kansa that as soon as each of their children was born he'd hand them over to Kansa.

Kansa ordered that his sister and his newly married brother-in-law be imprisoned until the death of their eighth child. As time passed Devaki gave birth to her first child who in cold blood was murdered by Kansa. Similarly, Kansa killed six of Devaki's children. During Devaki's seventh pregnancy Lord Vishnu moved the fetus from Devaki's womb to Rohini's womb in Gokul and so it was declared that the child died as a result of a miscarriage. During Devaki's eighth pregnancy Kansa made sure that the security arrangements were maximum to make sure that he could kill the child but the Lord had thought otherwise. On the day of his birth, it was made to rain very heavily. He also insight a deep magic which put all the guards to sleep. At birth, he showed his parents his divine form and then took the form of a normal human baby and gave instructions to be carried to Gokul.
