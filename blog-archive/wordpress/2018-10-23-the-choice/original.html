नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।



Lord Krishna had reached Dwaraka and was in a state of yoga-nidhra. Arjuna and Duryodhana made their ways see Shri Krishna. As Duryodhana saw Arjuna he raced into the room where Lord Krishna was resting and took a seat near the Lord's head. Arjuna took a seat near the Lord's feet as a mark of respect. As Shri Krishna awoke from his sleep, he instantly noticed Arjuna and greeted him. Duryodhana immediately interrupted the greeting and said that he was there first and it is only fair that he be greeted first. So Shri Krishna gave both of them a proper greeting and asked them what had brought both of them so far to Dwaraka. Both of them told Krishna that they seek his assistance in the situation of an impending war. Krishna thought for a while and came up with two solutions to the situation. Krishna offered that he would give one Akshauhini* from his Narayani-Sena and the second choice being he would offer himself to one side but would not fight. Duryodhana was given the first choice and even without thinking about the options chose the single Akshauhini. When Shri Krishna asked Arjuna what he wanted Arjuna humbly replied that he would have chosen Krishna and his side regardless of whether he would wield his weapons or not. Duryodhana has a hearty laugh to Arjuna's reply, gives his thanks to Krishna for the help and returns to Hastinapur.

*An akshauhini  is described as a battle formation consisting of 21,870 chariots ( ratha); 21,870 elephants ( Gaja); 65,610 horses ( turaga/ashwa) and 109,350 infantry ( pada sainyam).

One elephant (Gaja), one chariot (Ratha), three horses (Ashwa) and five foot- soldiers (Padhata) form a Patti;

Three Pattis form a Sena-Mukha;

Three Sena-Mukhas make a Gulma;

Three Gulmas a Gana;

Three Ganas a Vahini;

Three Vahinis a Pruthana;

Three Pruthanas a Chamu;

Three Chamus an Anikini;


Ten Anikinis form an Akshauhini. Thus an Akshauhini, by calculation, contains 21,870 elephants, 21,870 chariots, 65,610 Horses, and 109,350 foot-soldiers.
