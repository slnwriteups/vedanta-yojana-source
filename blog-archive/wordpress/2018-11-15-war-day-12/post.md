# War- Day 12

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो** **जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

With the failure in execution of the plan of capturing Yudhishthira Drona came to a conclusion that as long as Arjuna was stationed anywhere near Yudhisthira the challenge to capture him would be very great. Hence he sent an order to the Samsaptaka who had an ongoing feud with Arjuna to lure Arjuna out to a different part of the battlefield as to keep him occupied and away from Yudhishtira's side, making it easier to capture him. As the war began the next morning, Arjuna was lured away to a different part of the battlefield. On the other hand, Bhagadatta, son of Naraksura and the heir to the throne of Pragajyothishpuram (modern-day Assam) was wreaking havoc atop his elephant Supratika. He defeated the likes of Bheema, Abhimanyu, and Satyaki with total ease as though they were a thorn beneath his elephant's foot. Arjuna soon arrived at the scene by noon after easily defeating the Samsaptaka clan. Arjuna had a fierce battle with Bhagadatta. Both of them were masters in using divine weapons. Both of them continuously matched each other and finally, Arjuna gained the edge and defeated the demonic figure, bringing the war to a close for the day.
