# Hidimba

**नारायणं** **नमस्कृत्य** **नरं** **चैव** **नरोत्तमम्** **।**

**देवीं** **सरस्वतीं** **व्यासं** **ततो** **जयमुदीरयेत्** **।।**

After escaping from the place of lacquer the Pandavas along with Kunti go into hiding in the forest in the accompanying area. They travel through the forest and as night fell that night they decide to stay at a certain point where they found a cave for shelter. Bhima decided to stay up and keep guard as his brothers and mother took rest. But what was unknown to the Pandavas was that the forest was inhabited by demons who were magically viewing their every move. Hidamba was the ruler of the forest and had immense magical powers with which he tricked anyone who entered the forest and devoured them. Hidamba had a sister by the name Hidimba who was also very powerful she had the magical ability to change into a beautiful maiden who would lure people to Hidamba’s lair where he would use his magic to kill them and devour them. As the Pandavas awoke the next morning Hidimba approached them to bring them to her brother's lair. Kunti sensed something wasn't right and told her children to stay alert of what was to happen. Hidimba took the Pandavas to Hidamaba who tried to use his magic on the Pandavas who were unaffected. Stunned that his magic wasn't working, Hidamba challenged Bhima to duel with the mace. The two battled and Bhima emerged victorious killing Hidamba.

Seeing Bhima's bravery and strength Hidimba begs Kunti to let Bhima marry her. Upon his mother's instructions, Bhima agrees to marry her but he places a condition that he would leave her as soon as she bore him a child. Within a year of their marriage, Hidimba gives birth to a baby boy who was named Gatothkacha. Gatothkacha grows to become a great warrior like his father and plays a crucial role in Mahabharata war where he gives his life to save his uncle Arjuna’s.
