# Kunti

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

**देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

Kunti was born to Surasena of the Yadava clan. Her birth name was Pritha and she was the sister of Vasudeva the father of Lord Krishna. Her father gave her to his cousin Kuntibhojha who didn't have any children and then she received the name Kunti.

Once Sage Durvasa had visited the royal court of Kuntibhojha. Being extremely impressed pleased by all the comforts, patience and devotion offered by Kunti, he offered her a mantra by which she could invoke any god of her choice and that God would bless her with children. Out of innocence and curiosity, she invoked Surya the Sun God who bound by the power of the mantra blessed her with a child. This child was no ordinary child it was born with sacred armor and earrings. Out of fear of what the public might say Kunti abandoned the child. She placed the baby in a straw basket covered the top and left it to float on the waters of the Yamuna. The child was found by Adhiratha the charioteer of Dhritarashtra. As he didn't have children of his own he brought the child home where he and his wife Radha raised him as their own child. This child would later be known as Karna.

Years later Kuntibhojha organized a Swayamvara for Kunti where she chose Pandu to be her husband and became the Queen of Hastinapur. At the time Pandu was the King of Hastinapur as his older brother Dhritarashtra was blind and was deemed unfit to rule. Soon after their marriage, Pandu went on a mission to expand the borders of Hastinapur where he married the princess of Madra, Madri, sister of Shalya.

Once Pandu had gone a hunting trip where mistakenly he shot an arrow at the Sage Kindama and his wife who had taken the form of deer to mate. The dying sage in his last breathes cursed Pandu that if he were ever to get intimate with his wife and made contact with her then he would die. As soon as he was cursed Pandu renounced his kingdom putting Dhritarashtra to rule, went into exile with his wives. He then went to meet sages to know how he could reach Heaven and how he could achieve salvation with them. Hearing his request all the sages said one cannot aspire to reach heaven without children. Pandu then expresses his despair at the prospect of dying childless. Kunti then shares her story of how she got a mantra from Sage Durvasa to invoke gods to beget children. They then used the mantra to beget three children. First, she invoked Yama the god of death and dharma who blessed her with Yudhistira who would be known for his honesty. Then she invoked Vayu the god of wind who blessed her with Bhima who would later be known for his might and brute strength. Lastly, she invoked Indra the king of all gods who blessed her with Arjuna who would later be known for his skill as an archer. Kunti then shares the mantra with Madri but on a condition that she only uses it once. Madri cleverly invokes the Ashwini Devatas gods of the stars who bless her with twin children Nakul and Sahadev.

One day Pandu forgetting the curse given to him by Sage Kindama made contact with Madri and as a consequence of the curse died. Upon performing Pandu's last rites Madri entrusts her children to Kunti and jumps into the pyre committing Sati as she thought herself to be responsible for Pandu's death. Bhishma then takes Kunti and the five Pandavas back to Hastinapur to train them to inherit the throne.
