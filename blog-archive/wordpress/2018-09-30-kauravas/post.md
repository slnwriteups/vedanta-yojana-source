# Kauravas

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

**देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

Impressed with Gandhari's dedication to her husband, Vyasa grants Gandhari a boon of having one hundred sons. She gets pregnant but the duration of her pregnancy continued for an unusually long period and when she heard the news that Kunti had given birth she beat her womb in frustration. Upon beating her womb, a cold grey mass of tissue fell out. The tissue showed no signs of life. She immediately implored Vyasa to help. Vyasa took the tissue, divided it into a hundred parts and put it in a hundred earthen pots filled with ghee to incubate. Gandhari further asked him if she could also have a daughter to which he made another part and left that also to incubate.

After two years of incubation, the first pot was opened. As soon as the first pot was opened all the palace dogs started to howl and it was also accompanied by roaring thunder. Bhishma, Vidura, and Vyasa saw this to be an ill omen and asked Dhritarashtra and Gandhari to get rid of this child as it may cause a destruction of catastrophic proportions.

Gandhari refused this saying it was her first born and she wouldn't abandon the child no matter what. This child would later go on to be known as Duryodhana.

Slowly one by one all the children emerged from their pots. The second child born was Dushasana the third being Vikarna and lastly, the daughter was named Dushala.

During Gandhari's extended pregnancy, Dhritarashtra had a child with Gandhari's tending maid. This child was born at the same time as Duryodhana and Bhima and was elder to all his paternal half-brothers and sister Dushala. This child was named Yuyutsu.

Vikarana and Yuyutsu were the only two of the Kauravas who followed dharma. By the end of the war, Yuyutsu was the only surviving son of Dhritarashtra.
