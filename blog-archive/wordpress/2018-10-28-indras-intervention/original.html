नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।


Karna upon birth was no ordinary child. He possessed a divine armor (kavacha) and a pair of divine earrings (kundala). As long as he had these in his possession, he was impenetrable to any weapon may it be of man, deva or asura. When Indra learned of the properties of these divine kavacha and kundala, he saw these as a threat to his son Arjuna. He knew he had to help his son to win the Mahabharata war which was fast approaching. He came with a plan to strip Karna of these divine ornaments so as to make him vulnerable to any weapon that Arjuna would use against him. 

One evening in the sandhya-kaalam (a time when the sun is setting) when Karna was offering argyam ( offering water to the devas while chanting the Gayatri mantra) Surya Deva appeared in front of Karna. Karna greeted his father and asked him what had brought him to pay the visit. Surya said he had come to warn Karna that Indra in the guise of a Brahmin would come and ask him for his kavacha and kundala. He told Karna to not give his divine ornaments to Indra as they were a part of his being and blessing from his father as a means of protection. Giving this warning Surya disappeared. As Surya had said a Brahmin appeared asking for bhiksha.

Karna was known to give Brahmins whatever they desired as charity. Karna asked the Brahmin how he could serve him. Indra appeared to be in deep thought and then asked Karna that he give him his kavacha and kundala. Karna in spite of the warning given to him by his father rips of his kavacha from his body and forces the kundala off of his ears and places it in the hands of Brahmin. Indra immediately reveals his true form and said that he was very impressed with Karna. He tells Karna that in spite of being warned by Surya of what was going to happen he stood by his beliefs and gave away a part of his own being. Indra then said he would give him a weapon that would make him invincible for his great deed. Indra gave him a weapon known as Shakthi. He taught Karna the mantra to invoke the weapon but told him that he could use this weapon only once and after that even if he tried to invoke the weapon, it would not come to his aid. Saying this Indra returned to Indraloka.
