# War- Days 9,10

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

On a ninth day, Arjuna and Bhishma once again engaged in a fierce battle once again. Both of them fought so well that the battle seemed like it could go either way. Again like the third day, Arjuna was presented with many opportunities to kill Pithamaha Bhishma but out of his affection to his grandfather couldn't force himself to do it. Krishna once again descended from the chariot and took a wheel which was lying on the ground. As Krishna took the wheel it started rotating as his Sudharshan Chakra would. Bhishma laid down his weapons and welcomed death with open arms as he considered it an honor to fall at the hands of the Lord himself. Arjuna once again consoled Krishna and took an oath that he would lay Bhishma to rest. As these words were uttered the conch was blown closing the war for the day.
Later that night, Shree Krishna devised a strategy wherein Shikandi would be placed in front of Arjuna. Seeing a woman in front Bhishma would lay down his weapons creating the opportunity to turn the war around.
As planned the war broke out on the tenth day. Arjuna once again came face to face with Bhishma. Both of them fought fiercely once again and when the time presented itself Krishna signaled to Shikandi to come in front of Arjuna. Bhishma realized that Shikandi was the second coming of Amba and hence dropped his weapons. Arjuna then struck arrows one by one till Bhishma laid on a bed of arrows. No sooner than Bhishma fell did the conch get blown signaling end of the day's war. All the Pandavas and remaining Kauravas came to see Bhishma in his final resting place. Bhishma said he was thirsty and asked Arjuna to give him some water. Arjuna immediately materialized the Parjanya-Astra and invoked Ganga from Patalaloka hence the stream was called Patala-Ganga. The water shot straight into Bhishma's mouth quenching his thirst. Both the sides paid their respects to their grandfather and returned to their respective camps. Yudhishthira stayed back with Shree Krishna and spent some time with his grandfather. During this time, Bhishma gave Yudhishthira the Nyaya-Shaastra, Neethi-Shaastra and told Yudhishthira how to rule the kingdom. Finally, Yudhishthira posed on last question to Bhishma on which god to pray to and who was the ultimate being who would grant Moksham. To this Bhishma for the first time recited the Vishnu Sahasranaamam and told Yudhisthira that Krishna was none other than Lord Sriman Narayana himself and if one prays to him, he can attain Moksham.
