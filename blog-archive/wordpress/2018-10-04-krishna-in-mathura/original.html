नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् । 
देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

After his multiple failures in his attempts to find and kill Krishna, Kansa sends his chief minister Akroora to Vrindavan to invite Krishna and Balarama to Mathura to participate in a Danur-Yagna accompanied with a wrestling competition with an intent to kill them in Mathura. 
Akroora was sent to Vrindavan where he talked to Nanda and Yashodha about the Danur-Yagna function that was taking place in Mathura and that the king had asked Akroora to personally escort Krishna and Balarama to Mathura to take part in the competition. When Yashodha heard this, she was strictly against sending Krishna to Mathura but Krishna was firmly determined to go and told his parents that he would accept the invitation and go to Mathura. Akroora was to stay the night in Vrindavan for the night and they would leave first thing in the morning. In the morning they began the journey to Mathura. By the evening they decided to spend some time by the banks of the Yamuna to relax before entering Mathura by the morning. As Akroora took a dip in the Yamuna he saw the divine form of Lord Vishnu in Vaikuntham along with Goddess Lakshmi and as he kept looking in awe he saw Lord Vishnu taking the form of Krishna and Balarama. Akroora then understood why Krishna was undertaking the journey to Mathura. By morning they reached Mathura. Upon reaching Mathura, Krishna and Balarama got off at the city gates claiming they wanted to see the city before they reached the stadium to participate in the competitions, and told Akroora to inform Kansa of their arrival. As they strolled through the city Krishna performed his first miracle when a deformed woman by the name Trivakra fell to his feet. As Krishna picked her up, he first placed the sole of his foot on Trivakra's foot. Upon doing this her facial and skin deformities first vanished and as he pulled her up her hunchback straightened and transformed her into a beautiful maiden. Trivakra then acted as their guide of Mathura. No sooner than Krishna had performed this miracle the sound of people screaming in the background was heard. When Krishna and Balarama went to inspect the situation they saw a chariot approaching in a distance and that there were guards whipping the public to clear the road. When the guards approached Krishna and Balarama with an intent to whip them Balarama caught a hold of all the whips and pulled them out of the guards' hands making a fool out of them. In the meanwhile, the chariot had caught up to the scene where the charioteer turned out to be the Prince of Vidharbha Rukmi. Krishna and Balarama stopped the chariot and asked that Rukmi apologizes for the behavior of his guards. Rukmi was infuriated with the ask and with a sword in hand Rukmi descended from his chariot to impose his will on Krishna and Balarama. When he tried to slash Krishna, Krishna held the blade with his bare hands and blade dug deep enough for it to start bleeding. Krishna then disarmed Rukmi and held his own blade against his throat when a voice sounded from the chariot asking him to stop. As Krishna looked at the chariot to see whose voice it was, he saw a young woman descending from the chariot. This young woman was Rukmini to whom Krishna would get married in the future. She begged Krishna to spare her brother's life and apologized on his behalf. Seeing Krishna's wounded hand she tore a piece of her own cloth and tied it to his hand to stop the bleeding. Krishna spared Rukmi's life but made sure that Rukmi would remember this encounter by trimming half of his mustache.
After this Trivakra took Krishna and Balarama to the stadium where the Danur-Yagna was taking place. Krishna went to the bow single-handedly lifted the bow, strung it and broke it leaving the crowd stunned. Kansa then devised a plan to kill Krishna the following morning during the wrestling competition. He calls the mahoot of his strongest elephant Koliyapeed and instructs the mahoot to make sure the elephant gets drunk with alcohol and is set free to go on a rampage thereby trampling Krishna and Balarama on sight. The following morning outside the stadium the elephant is set free by the mahoot where it starts to go on a rampage. To stop any damage from taking place Balarama caught the elephant by the tail and starts to rotate it and hurls the elephant high in the air. In the midst of the wrestling competition, the elephant comes and lands in the stadium with a huge thud dying on spot. Krishna and Balarama participate in the wrestling contest and beat Kansa's best wrestling brothers Chanura and Mushtika. Soon Kansa himself faces Krishna in hand-to-hand combat but proves to be no match to his nephew. Lord Krishna slays Kansa and frees his parents and his grandfather from prison.
