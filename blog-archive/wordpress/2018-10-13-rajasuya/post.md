# Rajasuya

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

After slaying Jarasandha the Pandavas invited all the kings of *Aryavratha* to attend the ***Rajsuya Yagna*** that was to take place at Indraprastha. As per custom of performing the Rajasuya Yagna, the first honor was given to the king who was considered most worthy of taking precedence among all those who had assembled at the Rajya - Sabha. Soon a question arose among all the assembled kings as to who would be given the first honor. When Bhishma was approached with this question he advised Yudhistira to give the honor to Shri Krishna, King of Dwaraka. Following the instruction, he instructed Sahadev to give the honor to Krishna. No sooner than the instruction was given to Sahadev, Shishupala, King of Chedi who despised Krishna broke out in laughter and called this ridiculous saying the person who was following the instruction was born of illegitimacy(insulting Kunti and the Pandavas) and the one giving the advice was born of ever declining high and low(insulting Ganga and Bhishma) . He went on to insult Krishna by saying the one who is being honored is nothing more than a mere cowherd. He then sighted numerous people present in the hall like Drona who was their teacher, Vyasa who was a learned sage, Kripacharaya who was the royal advisor, Karna who was the disciple of Lord Parashurama and so on. He went on and gave a hundred insults to Krishna. Upon the hundredth insult that left his mouth, Krishna raised from his chair and summoned his *Sudharshana Chakra* and cut off Shishupala's head from the rest of his body. He then narrated the tale of Shisupala in the royal court. When Shishupala was born in the royal family of Chedi, he born as a freak of nature with four arms, three eyes and a scream which sounded like a braying ass. Seeing this his father Damaghosha the then king of Chedi summoned the royal priests to come up with a solution to the problem that they were faced with. The royal priests advised the king to abandon the child as it is a sight of ill omens. As the priests said this, a voice suddenly boomed from the sky saying the child would bring no harm to the kingdom and even if he was abandoned he would not die as he is destined to die at the hands of one who was born to slay him. His mother Sutasabha, sister of Vasudev asked the voice anxiously how she would recognize the one who would kill her child. The voice answered back saying when the baby is placed in the lap of the person, all his disabilities would disappear thus she could recognize the person who would kill her son. Soon afterward the hunt began to find someone who would heal the baby and in doing so would eventually be his undoing. As any guest entered the palace of Chedi, the baby would be placed on the lap of the person. Many days passed by but nobody was able to heal the baby. On one such occasion, Shri Krishna came to visit his aunt who was weeping. When he asked her why she was weeping, she narrated the entire story of her child. To this Krishna calmly asked her to place the baby in his lap. She immediately brought the baby and placed it in Shri Krishna's lap and no sooner than she had placed the baby in his lap that all the baby's disabilities disappeared. Sutasabha then asked Krishna for a small favor. She asked that Krishna pardon up to a hundred mistakes committed by her son. Krishna granted her wish and also told her that as soon as the hundredth mistake was committed, he would kill Shishupala. Known to us now is the fact that Shisupala was a reincarnation of Jaya one of the personal bodyguards to Lord Vishnu who was cursed by the four Kumaras to be separated from the Lord. After narrating this story the Lord summons back his Sudharshana but accidentally gets cut by his own weapon. Draupadi immediately tears off a piece of her saree and ties up the wound to stop the bleeding. Impressed by her devotion Krishna tells her that he shall forever protect her and all she must do in the time of need be to remember him.

Thus the Rajasuya Yagna proceeds and Yudishtira is recognized as the Emperor of Indraprastha.
