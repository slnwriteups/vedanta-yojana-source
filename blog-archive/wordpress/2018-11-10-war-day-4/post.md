# War- Day 4

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

With the start of day four, Bhishma went on the offensive. Ashwathama, Bhurisravas, Shalya, Chitrasena and the son of Chala together attacked Abhimanyu. Arjuna saw this joint attack and along with Drishtadyumna, joined the fight and evened up the odds that were against Abhimanyu. The son of Chala was killed in this fight. After his son's death, Chala himself joined the fight and made a fierce attack on Drishtadyumna. Shalya severed Drishtadyumna's bow leaving him at their mercy. At this point, Bheema charged at Chala and killed him. Shalya then ordered an elephant stampede against Bheema. Bheema with his iron mace dismounted his chariot and charged against the elephants and hurled them in different directions. The elephants fell on different soldiers, horses, and rathis, crushing them. Eight of the Kaurava brothers came to attack Bheema together but no sooner than the attack had begun that they had reached the gates of Yamalok. Duryodhana mourned the death of his brothers and in a fury attacked Bheema. Duryodhana attacked with a flurry of arrows. The arrows severed Bheema's bow and a second arrow pierced through Bheema's armor and got to Bheema's chest. Bheema was thus taken off the battlefield for the day. Gatothkacha grew furious by the attack on his father that he went on a fury and attacked the Kaurava army. By the time all these events were taking place, the sun had begun to set. Bhishma and Drona strategized that Gatothkacha and his powers were increasing as the sun was setting and thus decided that it would be wise to deal with Gatothkacha when he was weaker and thus with the consultation of Duryodhana blew the conch, bringing the war to a close.
