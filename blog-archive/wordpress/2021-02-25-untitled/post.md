# (untitled)

One gram of the Sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3) mixture is taken and weighed. Similarly, twenty milliliters of Hydrochloric acid (HCl) is taken in a beaker and weighed. The masses of the two reactants are then summed up and assumed to be the initial reactant mass. The Hydrochloric Acid is then added to the Sodium bicarbonate mixture, and the two are allowed to react together completely undisturbed. The mass of the Sodium bicarbonate- HCl mixture is measured. The same process is repeated thrice to establish three trials. Finally, the lost mass is calculated in the form of lost Carbon di-oxide( CO2).
