# War- Day 11

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो** **जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

After Bhishma’s untimely removal from the battlefield, Karna was given an opportunity to take part in the war. But the Kaurava army was in a serious dilemma as they had lost their supreme commander. Duryodhana and Karna met together with all the remaining leaders of the allied army forces to decide who would succeed Bhishma as the new supreme commander of the Kaurava Army. Chants of Karna’s name were heard among the ranks. Karna then spoke saying he was just one among the greats who have assembled to take part in the war. He suggested that Drona be installed as the supreme commander as he was the guru to both the Pandavas and the Kauravas hence he would know how to deal with a given situation on ground zero. It was agreed and Drona was made the supreme commander. Drona would go on to lead the army for the next five days before being killed by Drishtadyumna.

Drona was a man of strategy. Duryodhana then asked his guru to focus on only one goal and that was to capture Yudhishthira alive at all costs. He told Drona it was fine even if he couldn’t assure them a complete victory, it would be enough if he captured Yudhishthira alive. Drona at first was elated to hear this from Duryodhana’s mouth as he loved the Pandavas equally as much as he did the Kauravas. Duryodhana then spoke to reveal his true motives. He said he seen enough of his family die on the battlefield and if Yudhishthira was captured, then he could end the war and pretend to return a part of the kingdom, have him gamble it off again and send for another thirteen years into the forest. Drona in his heart cursed Duryodhana but was complient to capture Yudhishthira and bring a close to the war.
War began the next day with the Kaurava army turning all of their focus towards Yudhishthira. The Pandava army was aware of their plans through their spy network. Hence to keep Yudhishthira protected at all times there was one or the other warrior in his surrounding looking after him. There werew fierce battles that took place that day. Abhimanyu once again showed his valor by fighting Kritavarma, Jayadrata, Paurava, and Shalya all at once and defeating them singlehandedly. This adversely affected the Kaurava army who started to lose courage. Drona then saw that Yudhisthira was left unattended to and took this opportunity to go and capture him. Drona in his golden chariot raced towards Yudhishthira but Arjuna who was keeping a close watch over the situation sped on to the scene from nowhere and sent a barrage of arrows keeping Dronacharya away from his older brother. Drona left the scene and decided to stop the war for the day as he felt the Kaurava Army had sustained heavy losses for the day. On that account, the conch was blown bringing day eleven to a close.
