नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।


देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

After successfully conducting the Rajasuya Yagna Yudhishthira was declared as the emperor of Indraprastha. This title was more than what Duryodhana could digest. He grew jealous of the Pandavas day by day and wanted the title for himself. With every passing day, the Pandavas became regarded as very good administrators and Yudhishthira was regarded as the very able king with the people of Indraprastha going around talking that Yudhishthira should become king of both Indraprastha and Hastinapur. Duryodhana approached Shakuni and expressed his desire to claim the throne of Indraprastha. Shakuni suggested that they play to their strengths and Yudhishtira's weakness by inviting them to a game of dice as Yudhishthira was very fond of the game but he wasn't aware of the tricks to win a game of dice. Also, his luck with the game was never good. Duryodhana then went to his father for his approval to send an official invitation for a game of dice. Dhritarashtra at first did not approve of his son's idea and told him to consult with Vidura as to what he thought to be the best decision as Vidura was regarded to be the wisest person in the family. Duryodhana promptly told his father that it was just a casual game of dice between him and his cousins and so going to Vidhra would be redundant. Dhritharashtra then told Duryodhana to proceed as he wished and so an invitation was sent to the royal court of Indraprastha inviting the Pandavas, their mother Kunti and their wife Draupadi to the palace of Hastinapur to play a game of dice.
