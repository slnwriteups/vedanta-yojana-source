# Duryodhana: Dissecting his Character Through His Alliances

An in-depth analysis of Duryodhana's character necessitates a closer look at his relationships with key figures such as Karna, Shakuni, Dushasana, Sishupala, and Ashwatthama. Each alliance offers a unique lens to understand the intricacies of Duryodhana's character.

1. Karna: The bond between Duryodhana and Karna is marked by mutual respect, recognition, and strategic alliance. Duryodhana validates Karna's skills when others denounce him due to his supposed low birth, crowning him as a king and securing a formidable ally. However, Karna's inability to protect Duryodhana during the encounter with the Gandharvas illuminates the limitations of their alliance, revealing that friendships anchored primarily on personal and strategic gains can sometimes fail in times of distress.

1. Shakuni: The relationship with his maternal uncle Shakuni highlights Duryodhana's cunning and manipulative aspect. Shakuni, aggrieved by the marriage of his beloved sister Gandhari to the blind Dhritarashtra, seeks the downfall of the Kuru dynasty. He uses Duryodhana as a pawn in his game, fuelling his nephew's ambitions and jealousy towards the Pandavas. He guides Duryodhana on a path that undermines ethical and moral principles, contributing to Duryodhana's growing disregard for righteousness. Their alliance reflects not only Duryodhana's susceptibility to manipulation but also his readiness to employ deceitful tactics for personal gain.

1. Dushasana: As Duryodhana's brother, Dushasana's relationship with Duryodhana is marked by unwavering loyalty. However, this loyalty often manifests itself in supporting Duryodhana's wrongdoings, including the public humiliation of Draupadi. Their bond showcases the detrimental effects of naive commitment, where kinship overshadows justice and righteousness.

1. Sishupala: Duryodhana's friendship with Sishupala, known for his blatant disregard of dharma, further mirrors his prioritization of ambition over righteousness. Their alliance reveals Duryodhana's willingness to befriend those who defy societal norms if it benefits his pursuit of power.

1. Ashwatthama: Duryodhana's alliance with Ashwatthama, a mighty warrior but a morally flawed individual, underscores his tendency to prioritize power over ethics. Ashwatthama's questionable actions during and after the war resonate with Duryodhana's own transgressions, reflecting his inclination towards might over right.

In summary, these relationships mirror Duryodhana's character, reflecting his ambition, strategic insight, susceptibility to manipulation, and disregard for ethical norms. They underscore the consequences of valuing personal ambition and loyalty over righteousness, providing a nuanced perspective into Duryodhana's journey from a prince to the principal instigator of a devastating war.
