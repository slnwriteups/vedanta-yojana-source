# The End

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

After Ashwatthama was dealt with, the Pandavas returned to Hastinapur where all the citizens were in anguish with the loss of the lives of the hundred princes and many other great warriors who had fallen in the Mahabharata war. The Pandavas along with Lord Shree Krishna went to the royal palace where Kunti, Dhritarashtra and Gandhari were waiting to greet them. Dhritarashtra called Bheema forward to give him a hug, but Krishna replaced Bheema with and iron statue of Bheema’s stature. As Dhritarashtra got a hold of that statue the killing of his hundred sons came into his head and the statue burnt to ashes by the power of his anger, envy and greed. In remorse of his doings he immediately starts crying saying he had committed a grave sin by killing Bheema. Lord Krishna then reveals that he had put a statue in Bheema’s place and that Dhritarashtra didn’t commit any kind of sin. Yudhishthira is then returned his part of the kingdom and is named as the king of the entire kingdom. Yudhishthira accomplishes great feats as king. He successfully performs the Ashwameda Yaga and unifies most of the country.
Fifteen years into Yudhishthira’s rule as king, Dhritarashtra, Gandhari and Kunti go to the forest to undertake meditation and ultimately give up their lifes. Soon after this even Shree Krishna’s time on the earth comes to an end and he too returns to Shree Vaikuntam.
Yudhishthira totally ruled as king for a span of thirty-six years before handing over the crown to Arjuna’s grandson Parikshith. Yudhishthira along with his brothers and Draupadi decided to visit the holy theertha-kshetras before making their ultimate journey to meet the lord. After successfully finishing their yatra, one by one all the brothers as well as Draupadi give up their lives and reach the lord.

**कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा
बुद्ध्यात्मना वा प्रकृतेः स्वभावात् ।
करोमि यद्यत्सकलं परस्मै
श्रीममन नरायणायेतिसमर्पयामि ॥**

**सर्वं श्रीकृष्णार्पणम् अस्तु।।**
