# Vishnu Dharmam - 06 - Yudhishthira Chandala Samwaadam

![](images/01-aec967-2a7ebb49335e4a868d521052556e237a.jpg)In our previous entry, we saw the concept of *tapas.* Now we will see a short story that summarizes everything we have seen so far. Yudhishthira was a very fair, just ruler of Indraprastha. To test his sense of dharma, Yamadarma is said to have taken the form of a *chandala*(beggar) and visited Yudhishthira at the royal palace. It was customary at the time not to allow beggars into the palace. Yudhishthira welcomed the beggar with open arms and performed all the due *athithi-sathkaaram*. After this, the two of them got into a heated debate as to who is lesser than the other. Yudhishthira tells the beggar that by the due karma that he had committed in his previous lives that he has had the pleasure of being born as a human. Caste is but a tag that is given in society. The beggar then replies, saying he is lesser than him in terms of money. Yudhishthira says money is nothing but a commodity that one may possess today but may lose it tomorrow. The debate similarly continues to the point of nearly no end. Finally, the beggar in a fit of rage says that he would leave the palace. Yudhishthira calmly replies saying if the beggar leaves without having a meal in the palace, it would be against dharma as he would not be fulfilling his duty to perform *anna dhaanam* to a person who had come to his doorstep seeking alms and feeds the beggar. The beggar then shows his true form as Yamadarma and tells Yudhishthira that he is impressed with his knowledge of dharma and his conduct. Saying this he blesses the king and returns to his kingdom.
