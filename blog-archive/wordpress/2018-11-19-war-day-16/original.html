नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।


On the sixteenth day, Karna took over as the commander-in-chief of the Kaurava army. Before he took to the battlefield Kunti had secretly met with her son asking that he spare the lives of the Pandavas as they were his younger brothers. Karna tells her that after the war she would still be left with five sons indicating that he would show mercy to all except Arjuna. 
As the war began, Karna showed his capability as a leader. He first had an encounter with Bheema. Bheema tried all techniques he knew with the bow and arrow but failed to make an impact. Karna had the chance to kill Bheema but broke his chariot wheel instead. He then fought with Nakula and Sahadeva and disarmed both of them in the art of sword battle and spared their lives and went on to battle Yudhisthira. Yudhisthira attacked Karna with a fierce vigor to take revenge for the insult of his brothers but was cheaply outclassed. Karna targeted Yudhisthira's crown and dismounted it off Yudhishtira's head and moved on to Arjuna. Shalya who was Karna’s charioteer was glad that he had spared the lives of his nephews but he also knew that Karna wasn't going to spare Arjuna’s life. So to demoralize Karna, Shalya at each and every attack on Arjuna, condemned Karna for not being good enough to kill Arjuna. The battle between Arjuna and Karna raged on through the entire day. In the meanwhile, Bheema went after Dushasana and broke his chariot. He then proceeded to drag Dushasana by his right hand and pulled it out of his shoulder. After this, he ripped Dushasana apart by his chest, drank the blood and took some of the blood in his palms and applied it to Draupadi’s hair. And so ended the sixteenth day of the war.
