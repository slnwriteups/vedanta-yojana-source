# War- Day 3

नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

On the third day of war, Bhishma formed the Garuda Vyuha (Eagle Formation) with Duryodhana in the centre of the formation. To counter this formation, Arjuna suggested that the Pandava Army form the Chandra Vyuha (Crescent Moon Formation) with Bheema at one horn and Arjuna at the other. Duryodhana was of the opinion that is Arjuna taken out, then they could easily beat the Pandava army. Thus the Kaurava army focused their attacks on Arjuna. Various weapons were hurled at Arjuna but to no avail. Arjuna defended himself and directed his attention towards Pithamaha Bhishma. Again Bhishma and Arjuna engaged in a fierce battle. Arjuna had multiple opportunities to defeat Bhishma but didn’t do so. This was disturbing Shree Krishna. Arjuna had disarmed Bhishma by breaking his bow on more than three occasions but couldn’t bring himself to kill his grandfather. Krishna out of anger dismounted the chariot and told Arjuna that it was his duty to fight for dharma and if he was not going to fulfil his duty, then he would break his vow and take part in the war and by virtue of which his first duty would be killing Bhishma. Krishna raised his Sudarshan Chakra and was ready to release it when Arjuna fell to Krishna’s feet and asked him to not break his vow and that he would fulfil his duty and start by attacking Bhishma. Bhishma in the meanwhile saluted Krishna and told him that it would have been an honour if Krishna had killed him but he would have an equal honour dying at the hands of Arjuna.
In another part of the battlefield Shakuni used all his forces to attack Satyaki. He used his forces to destroy Satyaki’s chariot. Abhimanyu rescued Satyaki and the both of them fight together in a single chariot and decimated Shakuni and his forces forcing him to retreat.
Bheema had called upon Gatothkacha and his forces to help. The two of them attacked Duryodhana head on but Bhishma came to his rescue. As soon as Duryodhana was rescued, Bhishma blew his conch bringing the war on day three to a close.
