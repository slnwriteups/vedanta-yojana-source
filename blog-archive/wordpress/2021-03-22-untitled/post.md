# (untitled)

The topic I have chosen to work with is gene editing and regulation. Gene editing is how DNA can be inserted, deleted, modified, or replaced into a cell of any living organism. This topic relates to Mendelian genetics, gene expression, and mutation in the broad sense of topics pertaining to BIO282.

The article talks about how the gene-editing technology CRISPR (Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats) can be used to achieve better and more desirable traits within a species in order to increase the food production capacity, thereby reducing the potential risk of food shortages in the future. In this conducted study, the CRISPR tool is used to remove fertility from the embryo. The otherwise sterile embryo is capable of all other bodily functions and can be born healthy. Although this technology has yet to show results in cows, goats, pigs, it has started to show results in rat populations that have been promising. It shows that this method can potentially be used to cultivate animals that produce milk, meat and fibre that can be used for human consumption.
