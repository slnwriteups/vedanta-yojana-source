नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

Yesterday we have seen the story of Varaha Avataram and how the Lord saved Bhoomi Devi from the grasp of Hiranyaksha and established a platform for Brahma to recreate the world. Today we will be looking at the story of Kapila Maharishi.

It is said that the Kardama Prajapathi who was an ardent devotee of Lord Vishnu once performed deep penance to please the Lord. At a certain point in time the Lord was impressed by the penance performed and decided to show himself. The Lord seated himself atop Garuda and went to see Kardama Prajapathi. It is said that, when Kardama Prajapathi saw Garuda, he heard the Samaveda mantras as Garuda flapped his wings. This can be seen as a personification of the fact that the Vedanta that is the essence of the Vedas is sitting atop the Vedas. Garuda is Bhagavatha Purusha who is personifying the Vedas and Lord Sriman Narayana who is riding atop Garuda personifies the Vedanta Tatva as the Purushottama who is being celebrated in the Upanishads or the Vedanta. As Lord Sriman Narayana descended he tells Kardama Prajapathi that in a few days, Swayambhuva Manu(स्वायमभुव मनु) will come looking for a suitable groom for his daughter Devahuti. When Manu approaches Kardama Prajapathi with the offer, Kardama Prajapathi should accept. Similarly the Lord also instructed Manu to give his daughters hand in marriage to Kardama Prajapathi and so the both got married. Soon as time passed Devahuti gave birth to nine daughters and a son who was an incarnation of the Lord. This boy was named Kapila. He gained maturity at a very young age and established Sankya Yogam(not to be confused with that of the Bhagavad Gita). He states that there are twenty-four tatvas that belong to Prakriti and a twenty fifth tatva that is purusha. He teaches his Mother Devahuti about the cycles of life and death and how to escape this cycle. From this we can see that our Sanathama Dharma establishes that there is no fixed age for a guru. A guru is one who posses knowledge of a certain subject and has the ability to make his shishya learn and grasp that concept.  
Devahuti then starts to practice Bhakthi yogam and reaches the Lord.
