नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् । 
देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।
When Draupadi arrives with the five Pandavas to meet Kunti, they inform her that Arjuna won alms, to which Kunti says, "Share the alms equally". This motherly command leads the five brothers to become the five husbands of Draupadi. 
With the Pandavas' survival revealed, a succession crisis was started. Upon the news of Pandavas' death at Varnavrat, the title of the crown prince had fallen to Duryodhana. Dhritarashtra invites the Pandavas to Hastinapur and proposes that the kingdom is divided. The Pandavas are assigned the wasteland Khandavprastha, referred to as unreclaimed desert. With the help of Krishna, Pandavas rebuilt Khandavprastha into the glorious Indraprastha.
Khandavprastha was cursed by Lord Indra and was totally infertile. The region was inhabited by Asuras, Nagas, and Rakshasas and was under the Naga ruler Takshak.

 When the Pandavas entered Khandavaprastha they asked help from their cousin, Lord Krishna. Krishna then invited Vishwakarma and Mayasura, the great architects to design the town just the way he had made Dwarka. Vishwakarma is the presiding deity of all craftsmen and architects. Son of Brahma, he is the divine draftsman of the whole universe and the official builder of all the gods' palaces. Vishwakarma is also the designer of all the flying chariots of the gods and all their weapons.

 Soon, the work began and all the forest that surrounded the place was cleared by wildfires. In the process, the tribe of Asuras, Nagas, and Rakshasas were either killed or they fled towards the west. After the Khandava forests were burnt, the Rakshasas and Nagas were all gone and the infertile land was restored into a beautiful city.
