# War- Day 2

नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।

After a long drawn first day, the general to the Pandava army, Drishtadyumna devised a strategy to keep the Kaurava army at bay. Arjuna would face off against Pithamaha Bhishma and keep his attention diverted during which the Pandava army would attack and dominate on the battlefield.

At daybreak the sound of conches was heard across Kurukshetra. The battle began once again with the Pandavas following the plan.Lord Shree Krishna directed Arjuna’s chariot towards Pithamaha Bhishma. Arjuna gave a display of his respects and the two began to battle. The Gods once again came down from the heavens to witness the battle between Bhishma and Arjuna. Both of them were evenly matched. The chariots were moving at such a speed that the only distinguishing factor was the flag atop each of the chariots. The battle raged and in one of Bhishma’s attacks, and arrow struck Lord Krishna in the chest. As Krishna removed the arrow from his chest, Bhishma took the opportunity to set his sights elsewhere in the battlefield. In the meanwhile in another part of the battlefield Drishtadyumna engaged Drona in a head-on battle. Drona successively outclassed Drishtadyumna in his attempts to disarm Drona. At first Drona destroyed Drishtadyumna’s bow and his chariot. Drishtadyumna them took a mace and set out on foot. Drona then destroyed the mace. Without loosing any of his bravery and valour Drishtadyumna drew his sword and advanced forward. Drona then disarmed Drishtadyumna’s sword as well. Bheema who was onlooking this fight immediately rushed to provide relief to his brother-in-law. He swooped Drishtadyumna onto his chariot and attacked Drona fiercely with a shower of arrows. Duryodhana who was centrally placed onlooking the entire day’s battle, sent relief to Drona in the form of the Kalinga Army. Bhima like a mad bull went on a killing spree and decimated the entire Kalinga Army.Bhishma set his sights on Bheema and tried to stop him. Satyaki intervened by attacking Bhishma’s charioteer at first. Satyaki first killed Bhishma’s charioteer and then destroyed the bolts that were attached to the chariot’s wheels. Bhishma immediately jumped on to one his horses and rode away. Bhishma went to Drona and decided that they had suffered large casualties and decided that it was time to close the war for the second day and thus blew their conch.
