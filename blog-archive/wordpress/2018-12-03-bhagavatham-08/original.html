नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।
Today we will be looking at the story of Kurma Avataram.

 To understand the purpose of Kurma Avataram we must first look at the backstory associated with it.

 Once there was a dancer who wanted to impress the goddess, Maha Lakshmi. The dancer performed a very intricate set of dances and soon the goddess appeared before her and presented the dancer with a garland. Soon after this, it is said that Sage Durvasa was passing by and saw the lovely garland on the neck of the dancer. The dancer offered the sage the garland and left the scene. It is said that the garland possessed a very fragrant smell that attracted bees. Soon the sage started to get irritated by the buzzing of the bees and was looking to dispose of the garland when Indra arrived atop his mount Airavata. Durvasa presented the garland to Indra. Airavata took the garland but instead of throwing it on Indra, crushed it beneath its legs. Durvasa was extremely angered by this insult and cursed Indra saying he and the world would lose all their wealth. As the phrasing of the curse was vague, all the wealth bearing substances and creatures such as the Kalpa Vriksha, Kamadhenu, and even Goddess Lakshmi disappeared. Indra never knew a world without getting whatever he desired. Hence to bring back all his wealth, he headed to Vaikuntham to ask Lord Sriman Narayana for advice. The Lord told Indra that in order to get his wealth back, he must churn the milky ocean until all his wealth bearing substances and creatures returned to him. Thus arrangements started being made to churn the milky ocean when a situation arose wherein there wasn't enough manpower to churn the ocean alone by the devas. Indra approached the Danavas for help and in exchange for the help, they could claim whatever extra came out of the churning process. 
Thus the Meru mountain was placed on the milky ocean, Vasuki king of the snakes became the rope and  Lord Sriman Narayana in the form of a turtle placed the Meru on his back to provide support while the churning went on. Many substances came out as a byproduct of the churning. The first product that appeared was the Haalahalam poison which was consumed by Lord Shiva.
 Then in no particular order came the Uchaishravas, Kalpa Vriksha, Kamadhenu, Chandra and Lakshmi. Finally after completing the churning process appeared Danavantri with a golden chalice containing Amrutham( The elixir of longevity). Both the Devas as well as the Danavas desired the elixir and hence the Lord took the form of Mohini and gave the elixir only to the gods. It was during this that Rahu, a demon disguised himself as a deva and tried to consume the elixir when he was caught in the act by Surya and Chandra. The Lord releases his Chakra and cuts him in such a way that the upper part of the body and lower part of the body became separate entities. The Lord noticed that a snake was slithering past and cut the snake also in a similar fashion and attached the tail to the upper part of Rahu's body and attached the hood of the snake to the lower portion of the body. This lower part of the body came to be known as Ketu.
