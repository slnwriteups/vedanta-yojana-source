# Trickery

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

As soon as Duryodhana returned to Hastinapur, news came in that Shalya was on his way to meet Yudhishthira to provide aid to the Pandavas for the upcoming war. Duryodhana somehow by hook or by crook wanted Shalya to fight on his side and hence came up with a sly plan. He got his men to intercept Shalya's army before they could reach Matsya Pradesh. His men told Shalya that the King was organizing a feast and that the king desired for him to have a meal and take rest before he would arrive to meet Shalya. Shalya accepted the hospitality and had his meal and rested and asked that he meet the king. To Shalya's surprise, Duryodhana showed up and questioned how the hospitality had been. Shalya said that the meal was really refreshing and that the king was very kind in showing hospitality. Duryodhana then revelead that the king was none other than himself and not Yudhishthira. He said Shalaya was bound to return the favor as he had had the salt of Hastinapur. In times of yore, having salt in a person's camp meant that they were indebted to pay a favor to the one who fed them. Shalya being a man of honor asks Duryodhana to name his price. Duryodhana then replies saying he wanted Shalya and his army to aid the Kauravas in the war. Shalya was gutted that he had to go up against his nephews but being a man of honor accepts that he was indebted and that he had to repay the favor for the hospitality that was bestowed upon him by Duryodhana.
