# Pandu and Dhritharashtra

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।**

**देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

Vichitravirya after his marriage lived happily for a brief period of time before his untimely death. As fate would have it he also died without an heir to succeed him to the throne. It was a time of dilemma in the royal household as there was no successor to the throne and Bhishma bound to his vow couldn't take Vichitravirya's place as king. Satyavati was in dilemma to bring forward an heir to rule the kingdom and fill the empty post of the king. Satyavati then remembered Vyasa her firstborn of Sage Parashara. As per his promise to his mother, he appeared when she was faced with a crisis. Satyavati told Vyasa of the impending crisis that the kingdom was faced with. As Vyasa was also a great yogi she asked him to help the queens to conceive by the power of Niyoga. Vyasa told Satyavati that he had just returned from the forest after performing deep penance and asked that he be given some time for his personal grooming. Satyavati, who was in a hurry to reach a solution staunchly refused and asked him to immediately perform the required ritual. Vyasa looked very ferocious and was rather unpleasant to look at. When he went to perform the Niyoga technique with Ambika, she was terrified and shut her eyes during the process. As a consequence, Vyasa said that the child that would be born would be blind. This child would come to be known as Dhritharashtra. Satyavati was again taken aback as a blind person couldn't be appointed as king. So she asked Vyasa to again perform Niyoga to Ambalika. As he performed the Niyoga technique Ambalika went pale. As a consequence, the child would be born very pale and very weak. This child would go on to be known as Pandu. Satyavati who was again alarmed asked Vyasa to perform the Niyoga technique with Ambika for the second time with the hope that the child that would be conceived on this attempt would be the child who could become a capable ruler. Ambika who didn't want to go through the process for the second time sent a maid in her place. The maid bravely underwent the process and the child who would be born would be one who was extremely knowledgeable and very capable but would never inherit the throne as he was born of a maid. This child would be known as Vidhura.
