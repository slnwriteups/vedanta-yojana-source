# Decisions to be Made: War or Peace

**नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम् ।

देवीं सरस्वतीं व्यासं ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ।।**

After Abhimanyu's wedding, Krishna and Balarama met with Yudhishthira, Drupada, Satyaki, and Virata in Virata's assembly hall. Krishna once again narrated the events that had taken place in the royal court of Hastinapur of how the Pandavas were cheated and how their kingdom was taken away deceitfully. He then went on to say that Yudhishthira had no desire of violence and that he wanted to settle all the impending issues with honor and dignity without spilling the blood of his loved ones. But what was not established was how Duryodhana might react to any offer that was to be made by the Pandavas. Balarama agreed with what Krishna said as he was a guru to both the Pandavas and the Kauravas and he too did not want to retort to war as a solution.
Balarama was also of the opinion that Yudhishthira was in no position to reclaim his kingdom as he was completely conscious of what he was doing when he gambled away his kingdom. Satyaki begged to differ and said what happened to the Pandavas was a matter of injustice and it is their moral obligation to help the oppressed in getting justice even if it meant war. This ideology was one that even Drupada shared. Drupada pressed that arrangements be made for war as he felt that Duryodhana wasn't a person who would listen to the reasoning of his elders and hence sent word to Shalya, Dhrishtaketu, and Susharma who had just been defeated at the hands of Virata to contribute towards the cause of the Pandavas. Drupada also sent his head priest to Hastinapur and instructed him to try and reason with Duryodhana and Dhritarashtra one last time before making war the only possible solution. Krishna further said that both the Pandavas and Kauravas are both close to them and hoped that Duryodhana would see the larger picture. The conference ended and Krishna along with Balarama headed back to Dwaraka. As soon as Duryodhana learned of these events that had taken place, he rushed to Dwaraka to gain assistance from Krishna. Arjuna also was sent to Dwaraka to ask for assistance.
