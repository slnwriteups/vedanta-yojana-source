Privacy Policy

Vedanta Yojana · Last updated September 22, 2026

Vedanta Yojana ("the app") is a reference app for Sri Vaishnava scripture, Divya Desam temple information, and daily Panchangam (Hindu calendar) details. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and how it is handled.

Location data

When you open the app's Home screen, it requests your device's approximate location to calculate today's Panchangam (tithi, nakshatram, upcoming Ekadashi) and Samkalpam for where you actually are, rather than a fixed city. This is the only use of location data in the app.

How it's used:

  • Your device's coordinates and configured timezone are sent to Sri Ahobila Mutt's public calendar services (hosted at ahobilamutt.org and its backing calendar API) solely to compute the correct Panchangam for your location.
  • Your coordinates are also turned into the name of the place they fall in (“Chennai”), so the app can show which place the day's Panchangam and Sankalpam are calculated for instead of an unnamed “your location”. In the Android app this lookup is done by your device's own operating-system geocoder; on the website it is done by BigDataCloud's public reverse-geocoding service (api.bigdatacloud.net), which receives the coordinates being named and nothing else — no account, no API key, and no identifier of any kind. The resolved name is cached on your device, so the lookup is not repeated for a place you have already been shown.
  • We do not use this data for advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it is never linked to your identity.
  • The result is cached on your device for the current day only, so the app does not request your location repeatedly.
  • If you decline the location permission, the app simply shows that Panchangam data is unavailable — every other feature works normally. If the place name alone cannot be resolved, the Panchangam is still calculated from your coordinates and is simply shown without a place name — never under a guessed one.

Data stored only on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Your reading progress (which chapter you last opened, per book)
  • Bookmarked chapters
  • Language, theme, and text-size preferences

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data. We have no server that stores or can access it.

App updates

The Android app checks for two different kinds of update, both over HTTPS:

  • Improvements to the app itself. On launch, the app asks Expo's update service (u.expo.dev) whether a newer version of its code has been published, and if so downloads it in the background so it runs the next time you open the app. The request identifies the app and which version of it your device is running — it carries no account, no advertising identifier, and nothing that identifies you. This is how fixes and improvements reach you without you having to install anything.
  • New app releases and Library content. The app asks our own website whether a newer installable version or new Library content is available, as it always has.

Aggregate counts, with no identifiers

Two things are counted in aggregate. Neither involves a cookie, an advertising identifier, a device identifier, or anything that could single you out or be linked to you.

  • The app's update and content checks. The app asks our website whether a newer version or new Library content is available — a check it has always made, and which is what tells you an update exists. Our website's network provider counts those requests and the country they came from, so we can see roughly how many people use the app and where. The count is of requests, not of people: we cannot tell one device from another, and we do not try to.
  • Visits to our website. The website at vedantayojana.org records aggregate page views and the visitor's country using a cookieless measurement service. It sets nothing on your device, stores nothing in your browser, and cannot follow you to any other site.

Neither source is used for advertising, sold, or shared for marketing purposes, and neither is ever linked to your identity.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or usage-tracking service is built into the app. The app itself measures nothing and reports nothing about how you use it. (For the aggregate request counts our website keeps, see above — they record no identifier and no individual behaviour.)
  • No data is sold or shared for marketing purposes.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
