## Translation checkpoint — 2026-11-05

**Translation coverage: 116/116 chapters (100%). Nothing remains to translate in Telugu.** This means the translation is complete. It does not mean the linguistic or theological review is done.

- **Scope:**
  - All 1,000 name explanations.
  - The introductory chapters and the Dhyana Shlokam.
  - The concluding shloka, Phala Shruti, Uttara Bhagam and conclusion.
  - The birth-star introduction and all 27 birth-star chapters.
- **Starting point:** 7 chapters plus the book title, description and part titles were already in Telugu. The remaining 109 chapters were added with engine batches 245–320. No existing Telugu field was replaced.
- **Translator:** `claude-te-2026-11-05`. One agent, Telugu only, following the order hi → ta → kn → te.
- **Mechanical parts:** Sanskrit verses, all 1,000 name lines and all 1,000 mantras were generated by the existing engine. Romanised Sanskrit quotations were converted through the engine's IAST → Telugu converter. Tamil quotations were transliterated from the Tamil text according to the Telugu glossary mapping.
- **Telugu conventions:**
  - The Lord is స్వామి / ఆయన, with singular verbs.
  - Āzhwārs and Āchāryas take honorific plural verbs.
  - Restrictive particles are not added where the English has none.
  - Western digits and straight quotes throughout.
  - No gloss echo.
- **Nakshatra names:** the usual Telugu forms (అశ్విని … రేవతి), proposed as new terms.
- **Location labels:** the A/B labels were localised as అ/బ, as in the Kannada edition.
