## Sources consulted

- Owner-supplied English volumes 1–5; volume 1 on the manifestations, volume 2 printed p. 18 on aravindakshah.

## Corrections

- Sentence boundary between destruction and Vibhava; paragraph count preserved.
- Wrong mouth quotation in the eyes explanation replaced with the eyes quotation from Amalanadipiran 8. Inherited Hindi corrected too.
- Mukunda identified as the Lord, not the name of a garland; English and inherited Hindi corrected.

- Book metadata follows glossary பலஸ்ருதி / நக்ஷத்திரம். Mobile catalogue regenerated.
- Actual chapter naama-352-361: ellipsis marks words omitted from the Tiruvaymozhi 4.5.1 citation; nothing silently joined as a continuous quotation.
- Actual chapter naama-362-385: restored மற்றை நிலமகள் பிடிக்கும் மெல் அடியை from Tiruvaymozhi 9.2.10, including corresponding English/Hindi quotation; restored மயக்குக்களால்.
