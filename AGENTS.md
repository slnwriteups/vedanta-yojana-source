# Instructions for AI coding and writing agents

This repository's working rules are in `CLAUDE.md`. They apply to every AI
tool, not only Claude. Read it first. In particular, never add, edit or
remove a What's New announcement (`content/announcements.json`) without
the owner's approval.

Before translating or transliterating any content into Tamil, Kannada,
Hindi or Telugu, read `docs/TRANSLATION-GUIDE.md` and that language's
glossary in `docs/translation-glossaries/`.
